The rules

Spell GOLD on the drop targets to light the lock in the center scoop. Three locks start multiball; ramps and orbits score jackpots, the scoop scores the super jackpot.

Spell MAP or make 4 ramps to light a mission at the scoop. Flip to choose. Finish all six to play Graveyard of the Atlantic.

Shots in a row are combos. SOS lanes raise the bonus multiplier. Hit the top lanes on the plunge for a skill shot; the right ramp right off the plunge is a super skill shot.

Graveyard of the Atlantic

Up to two players: press Start again on ball 1 to add player 2. Xbox controllers: LB/LT and RB/RT flip, hold A (or pull the right stick down) to plunge, flick the left stick to nudge, Menu starts and pauses.
