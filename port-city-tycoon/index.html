Port City Tycoon

Buy up Wilmington from Castle Street to Wrightsville Beach, collect rent, and survive the Court Docket. Run short of cash and you can file Chapter 13 once to keep your holdings.

How to play

House rules

  1. Everyone starts with $1,500 and collects $200 each time they pass Payday.
  2. Land on an unowned property to buy it. Own a whole color set to double its rent and start building. Build evenly: no street gets a second house until every street in the set has one.
  3. Tap any deed another player owns to make an offer. Bots will also try to buy the missing piece of a set from you. Nothing with buildings on its set can change hands.
  4. Doubles earn another roll. Three doubles in a row lands you in County Jail. Get out with $50 bail, a Charges Dropped card, or doubles within three turns.
  5. Short on cash? Sell buildings back at half price or mortgage property for half its value. Mortgaged property collects no rent.
  6. Still short? Chapter 13 keeps all your property: what you couldn't pay, plus a 10% trustee fee, comes out of your next three paydays. Building is frozen until discharge, and missing a plan payment converts the case to Chapter 7. You get one Chapter 13 per game.
  7. Chapter 7 ends your game. Your property and remaining cash go to the creditor you owed, or back to the bank.

Xbox controller: D-pad or left stick moves the highlight, A selects, X takes the main action (roll, buy, end turn), B closes, Y shows these rules.
