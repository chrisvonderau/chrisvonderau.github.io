Grown-up plan for feeding time (screen off)
  • Before the feed: sit together, tap Show Emmy, then put the iPad away.
  • Feeding basket: a basket of quiet toys that only comes out while Everett eats. Rotate the toys weekly so it stays special.
  • Helper job: Emmy brings the burp cloth or the pillow. Say "Thank you, helper!"
  • During: talk to her in 1 to 2 words: "Basket!", "Everett eats.", "Almost done."
  • After: keep the promise right away: "All done. Hug!"
  • Use your own photos (Grown-up corner, Photos) of the real basket and Everett.

Grown-up corner

How to use it

  1. Together, on your lap. 3 to 5 minutes, then All done. It is a practice tool, not a babysitter.
  2. Model the word: "Help!"
  3. Wait 5 seconds and look at her expectantly. The bar on the big button counts it for you.
  4. Prompt if needed: say it again, or show the sign.
  5. Praise any try ("eh", "hep", a sign): tap She said it! right away and give her what she asked for.
  6. Then practice for real with the drills below. Real life matters more than the screen.

Her own taps on the big button just say the word out loud (like a talker button). Only She said it! counts as a win.
