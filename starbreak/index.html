Defend the last station. Fling asteroids. Survive the Harrow.

HOW TO PLAY

Move — iPad/iPhone: drag anywhere (the ship moves with your finger, it never jumps). Mac: just move the mouse, or use arrows / WASD.

Shooting is automatic. Aim by lining up under enemies.

PULSE (button / click / Space) clears nearby bullets, shields you for a moment and flings asteroids. Glowing asteroids smash enemies and set off chains for a score multiplier — and can’t hurt you.

Divers flash before they swoop. Breachers show a red lane — stop them before they hit the station. Hit a warden to drop its shield bubble.

Mac keys: P/Esc pause · M mute · R restart

CHOOSE YOUR DIFFICULTY

First time defending the outpost? Easy gives the station more armour (16), slows enemy fire and recharges your PULSE in 5 s.

You can change this any time in Settings. Easy and Normal keep separate best scores.
