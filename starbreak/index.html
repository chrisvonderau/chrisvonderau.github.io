HOW TO PLAY

Move — iPad/iPhone: drag anywhere (the ship moves with your finger, it never jumps). Mac: just move the mouse, or use arrows / WASD.

Shooting is automatic. Aim by lining up under enemies.

PULSE (button / click / Space) clears nearby bullets, shields you for a moment and flings asteroids. Glowing asteroids smash enemies and set off chains for a score multiplier — and can’t hurt you.

Divers flash before they swoop. Breachers show a red lane — stop them before they hit the station. Hit a warden to drop its shield bubble.

Power-ups drop from enemies — fly into a capsule: spread shot, a stored second pulse, or a wingman. Kills earn scrap to spend in the upgrade bay between waves.

2 players (co-op) — on the title pick 2 PLAYERS (or press 2), then press A on controller 2. Controller 1 (or keyboard, mouse, touch) flies the cyan ship, controller 2 the amber one. Score and scrap are shared; a player who runs out of ships comes back with the next extra ship or a REVIVE in the upgrade bay.

Mac keys: P/Esc pause · M mute · R restart · F full screen

Xbox controller — left stick moves (push gently to creep, all the way for full speed) or D-pad · A pulse · Start pause · B back · Y mute · View restart · hold the left stick in for a second for full screen. In menus, the D-pad picks and A presses.

CHOOSE YOUR DIFFICULTY

First time defending the outpost? Easy gives the station more armour (16), slows and softens enemy fire, gives you longer to recover after a hit and recharges your PULSE in 5 s.

You can change this any time in Settings. Easy and Normal keep separate best scores.
