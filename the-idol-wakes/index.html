HOW TO PLAY

You walk the jungle into a buried sanctum, lift the relic, and run when the stone god wakes. The route is yours to steer. Nothing moves you forward unless you do.

  • WASD or arrows — move. Drag the right side, or a locked pointer, to look.
  • Space — jump. Timing is generous: a moment after leaving a ledge still counts, and a press just early still lands.
  • Shift or C — slide. Low gaps and falling beams. You keep your speed.
  • Jump into a vine to catch it. Space releases. Holding on is safe; letting go early is faster.
  • Jump at a marked wall to run it. Jump again to leap off.
  • Jump into a broken ledge and you mantle up.
  • Q / E, or a quick double-tap of A / D — dodge. On a touch screen, swipe the right side.
  • F — Relic Sight. A short pulse that lights hidden ways farther ahead.
  • Esc — pause. On a touch screen, use the pause mark.

An Xbox controller uses the same actions. Prompts change to the controller when it was used last, and back when you use the keyboard, mouse, or touch. Look, invert, and vibration for the controller are in Settings.

The relic slows you a little and makes shortcuts glow: cracked walls, side vines, quiet ledges. They are faster and less forgiving. Three lives. A fall, a fatal trap, or the idol itself returns you to the last checkpoint — until the last life, which begins the chapter again.

Par time is the gold line. Under par, with every shortcut and every shard, the sanctum names you Idol-Master.
