    How to play

    1. Race all 10 of your marbles across the star into the point directly opposite your home.
    2. On your turn, move one marble: either step into an adjacent empty hole, or jump over an adjacent marble (anyone's) into the empty hole right behind it.
    3. Jumps chain. Keep hopping as long as there's a marble to vault, in any direction, and stop on any landing you like. A step and a jump can't be combined.
    4. Once a marble reaches the goal it may shuffle within the goal but can't leave it.
    5. No stalling: if the goal is completely full and at least one marble there is yours, you win.
    6. Trackpad: click a marble, then a glowing hole, or drag and let go. Pinch to zoom, swipe with two fingers to pan, and press FIT to reset the view. On a phone, pinch and drag the board the same way.
