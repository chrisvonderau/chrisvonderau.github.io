HOW TO PLAY

Mac or iPad with a trackpad. Click Start; the pointer locks to aim.

iPad or iPhone (landscape). Aim Assist is on by default for touch.

Xbox controller (one or two). Pair it in Bluetooth settings, then press any button on it. A second controller joins as Player 2 by pressing A or ☰.

LS Walk
RS Look and aim
RT Fire
LT Hold for steady aim
X Reload (again on the ring: perfect)
B / LS Hold B or click LS to run
Y Quick turn around
A Skip / confirm
☰ Pause
D-pad Menus: A select, B back

Your stovepipe bazooka holds one rocket. After every shot you must reload. Tap RELOAD again as the brass ring closes for a perfect reload.
Head or body hits destroy a zombie. Leg hits knock it down. Two bites and you're done.
