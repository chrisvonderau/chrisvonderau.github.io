How to play

Two players, 8 marbles each. Sapphire (blue, star) starts on the left and moves first. Amber (orange, ring) starts on the right.

Your turn

  • Click one of your marbles, then a glowing hole.
  • Slide one space up, down, left or right. No diagonals.
  • Jump straight over one neighbouring marble into the empty hole behind it. Jumping an opponent captures it. Jumping your own is allowed and captures nothing.
  • After a jump you can keep jumping with the same marble. Stop whenever you like with End turn (or click the marble again).
  • If any capture is available, you must take one. A red ring marks a capturing jump.

Winning

  • Capture every opposing marble, or
  • End your turn with 4 marbles in your goal: your opponent's starting area (the shaded zone across the board). With fewer than 4 left, you need all of them, but at least 3.
  • If your opponent has no legal move on their turn, you win.

Draws

The same position three times, or 100 turns in a row with no capture and no new best in either goal.

Arcade bonuses (points only)

Turn on in Settings. Captures score 100, extra jumps in a chain 50, and a chain of 3+ jumps is a Triple Jump worth 500. Captures fill your meter. When it's full, press Frenzy to double your points for 3 turns; capturing 2 at once starts Frenzy for free. Points never decide the winner.

Keys

Esc cancel · Enter end turn · ⌘Z or Ctrl Z undo

Settings

Opponent, rules and arcade changes start a new game.
